# Nexus Autocomps Website

## Scope
Build the complete responsive manufacturing website described in the uploaded brief, with distinct pages for Home, About Us, Services, Products, Gallery, Blog, and Contact.

## Experience
- Establish a premium industrial system using graphite, steel neutrals, a restrained safety-orange accent, strong condensed headings, fine borders, and subtle motion.
- Create a sticky navigation that overlays the home video initially, becomes solid while scrolling, and supports an animated mobile menu.
- Use a cinematic manufacturing video with a strong poster fallback as the first home-page visual.
- Populate every media area with realistic generated manufacturing imagery; no empty or broken placeholders.

## Pages and interactions
- **Home:** video introduction, company overview, service cards, interactive manufacturing-process timeline, filterable products, filterable lightbox gallery, precision benefits, article previews, and enquiry call-to-action.
- **About:** company overview, approach, manufacturing philosophy, mission, and vision.
- **Services:** detailed sections for all six manufacturing capabilities.
- **Products:** filterable component catalogue with centralized sample data.
- **Gallery:** responsive categorized gallery with accessible lightbox viewing.
- **Blog:** realistic engineering article listing.
- **Contact:** validated enquiry form with clear success feedback; submissions remain frontend-only in this first version.
- Add a shared footer, consistent calls-to-action, active navigation states, and route-specific search/social metadata.

## Technical approach
- Keep media references and sample content centralized for easy future replacement.
- Build reusable page sections and cards with React, TypeScript, TanStack Router, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide icons.
- Use semantic design tokens in the global stylesheet, lazy-load non-critical images, and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Verify the live site at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation, filters, timeline, lightbox, form validation, media playback/fallback, and page metadata.
